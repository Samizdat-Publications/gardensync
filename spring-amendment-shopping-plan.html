Application Plan

First — A Note About The Clover

From the photo, those beds are full of chickweed and clover — that's a good thing. Don't pull it all out. Clover is a nitrogen fixer and chickweed is decent green manure. Chop it in with a hoe or shovel before you add amendments. Free organic matter + nitrogen boost. Remove only the dandelions and any tough perennial weeds (quack grass, thistle).

◆ SATURDAY · APR 18 — AFTERNOON
Prep The Beds
  • Chop in the clover/chickweed. Hoe or shovel — turn the green stuff into the top 2–3". Let it wilt 30+ min before covering.
  • Remove dandelions (tap root and all) and any woody perennials.
  • Break up any soil crust with a rake. Don't deep-till — you want to preserve soil structure.
◆ SUNDAY · APR 19 — AMEND DAY
Mix & Apply (All 5 Beds)
Per-bed recipe (mix in wheelbarrow first):
  • 2.6 cu ft compost (~⅓ of a contractor's wheelbarrow)
  • 0.8 cu ft perlite (~6 heaping shovelfuls)
  • 3 cups (1.5 lb) Espoma Garden-tone
  • ½ cup blood meal
  • ½ cup bone meal
  • ¼ cup kelp meal (if you have it — otherwise add next weekend)
Apply: Spread blend across the bed, rake into top 1–2". Don't bury deep.
Water in: 2 gallons of Miracle-Gro solution per bed (1 tbsp per gallon). This kicks the microbes awake and starts dissolving the granular nutrients.
NEXT · APR 26 – MAY 3
Cool-Season Planting
Direct-sow peas, spinach, lettuce, radish, carrots, beets, kale. Transplant brassicas (broccoli, cabbage) if you have starts. Don't add more fertilizer yet — you just loaded the beds. Water with plain water.
MAY 10 – MAY 17 (AFTER FROST)
Warm-Season Transplants
Tomatoes, peppers, squash, beans, cucumbers go in. Drop a pinch of bone meal in each planting hole for root development. Water in each transplant with 1 gal Miracle-Gro solution.
EARLY JUNE
First Side-Dress
Sprinkle 1 cup Garden-tone around the base of each heavy-feeder (tomatoes, peppers, squash, corn). Scratch in lightly, water. Foliar-spray kelp meal tea (¼ cup kelp in 2 gal water, steep 24 hr, strain) — micronutrient boost.
JULY · FLOWERING / FRUITING
Bloom-Time Push
As plants flower and set fruit, they need more P and K. Second Garden-tone side-dress (another 1 cup per plant). The bone meal you added in April is still breaking down — now it's releasing when plants need it most. Foliar kelp again if you have it.
AUGUST
Final Boost & Succession
Last Garden-tone side-dress for late-season heavy feeders. Start fall crops in empty bed space (kale, radish, lettuce, spinach). Keep watering — August drought stress is what usually ends the season early in Canton.
OCTOBER · BED CLOSE-OUT
Cover Crop
After harvest, broadcast crimson clover or winter rye across any empty beds. It'll sprout in fall, survive winter, and chop in next March — same trick you're using this weekend, but planned this time. No fertilizer needed to close out.
