Tomato

Solanum lycopersicum Nightshade family, with peppers, eggplant and potatoes
  • Killed by frost
  • Full sun
  • 1 per square, staked
  • Pot of 5 gallons or more
  • About 10 lb a plant

When, in Portland

  1. Feb 17 to Mar 17Start seeds indoors6 to 8 weeks before you plant out, Apr 14 to Apr 28
  2. Apr 14 to Apr 28Plant outGuides say 1 to 4 weeks after your last frost (Mar 22). Held back to Apr 14, when frost has passed in 9 years out of 10, because tomatoes die at freezing.
  3. From Jun 23Start pickingAbout 70 days after planting out, until frost around Nov 21

Good and bad neighbours

French marigold: its roots suppress root-knot nematodes. Shown in trials.

Basil: shares water and sun needs; said to improve flavour. Traditional, not proven.

Potato, keep apart: the two share late blight and other diseases. Shown in trials.

Growing it

Start seeds indoors 6 to 8 weeks before you plan to plant out, 1/4 in deep, at 70 to 80 °F. Plant out once nights stay above 50 °F, burying the stem up to the first true leaves.

Stake or cage at planting. Water deeply and evenly to prevent blossom-end rot, and mulch once the soil is warm.

Sources: University of Minnesota Extension, Royal Horticultural Society
