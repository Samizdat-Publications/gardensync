EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

GardenSync is a free, open-source community garden planner built specifically for Food Not Bombs Canton, OH. It features three research-backed garden plans optimized for food bank donations from 5 raised beds (4'×8' each, 160 sqft total) in USDA Zone 6a.

The app includes an AI-powered Garden Buddy (Claude Sonnet 4), companion planting intelligence, harvest tracking, volunteer management, and cloud sync — all in a zero-dependency vanilla JavaScript application with a punk/anarchist aesthetic.

FNB FULL RESEARCH PLAN

5 BEDS · 400-600 LBS · 22 CROP TYPES · FULL COMPANION PLANTING · 3 VOLUNTEER CREWS

The flagship plan. Research-backed crop selection optimized for: harvest window tolerance (no daily picking required), food bank shelf life (60%+ no-fridge), and yield per square foot. Key exclusion: zucchini (#1 unwanted food bank donation + vine borer magnet), corn (poor sqft yield), cucumbers (daily harvest needed).

FNB EASY START

5 BEDS · 130-225 LBS · 12 CROP TYPES · 100% LOW-MAINTENANCE

Designed for first-year community gardens with unreliable volunteer schedules. Every crop in this plan is rated low-maintenance — plant once, water occasionally, harvest when ready. No staking, no daily picking, no fussy succession planting.

WHY EASY START?

Most community garden projects fail in Year 1 due to volunteer burnout from high-maintenance crops. Easy Start eliminates that risk:

FNB MAX STORAGE

5 BEDS · 250-400 LBS · 9 CROP TYPES · 70%+ SHELF-STABLE

Optimized for no-fridge pantry distribution. When your food bank doesn't have cold storage, this is your plan. Potatoes, onions, garlic, squash, and dried beans — crops that store 3-8 months in a cool dark room.

STORAGE CAPACITY

Two full potato beds provide staggered harvests from July through October, with stored potatoes lasting through March. Combined with alliums and squash, this plan provides nearly year-round food security from 160 sqft.
