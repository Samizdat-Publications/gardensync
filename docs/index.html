Sec.02

The workspaces

6 tabs
01

Bed Planner

Drag or click plants onto a square-foot grid. Spacing comes from real agronomy — 16 carrots per square, one tomato per 2×2 ft — and companion threads draw themselves between friends while antagonists flag red. Seven container types, from raised beds to whisky half barrels.

02

Grow Schedule

A Gantt chart of the whole season built from what you actually planted, colour-coded for start-indoors, transplant, direct sow and harvest. Every date is calibrated to Canton's last frost of April 18 and first frost of October 28.

03

Harvest Log

Record what you pick, in pounds, per bed, with a flag for what went out to the community. Set a season goal and watch the bar fill. Season insights surface your top producer, best bed, average days between picks and donation rate.

04

Volunteers

A crew list with availability levels, bed assignments and a generated weekly task list — water checks and weed patrol, per bed, per person. Built for spotty availability, because mutual aid runs on real life rather than corporate schedules.

05

Climate Data

Live conditions and a 7-day forecast from Open-Meteo, with a status readout that tells you whether it's an ideal growing temperature and how many days remain until first frost. Rainfall and temperature curves are drawn on canvas.

06

Visualize

Generate an AI mockup of one bed or the whole garden through Google Gemini, to see roughly what a layout will look like before anything goes in the ground. Bring your own key.

Sec.06

Under the hood

No build step
No tooling
No bundler, no framework, no npm. Every module is a plain <script> tag and communicates through one shared global state object. Clone it and open the file.
Drawn, not templated
Beds, companion threads, climate curves and harvest charts are all drawn programmatically on <canvas> or inline SVG. No D3, no Chart.js.
Yours to keep
Autosaves to localStorage, with optional Supabase cloud sync. Export JSON, save a PNG, or copy a share link that folds the entire garden into the URL. Full undo/redo.
Offline-first
Everything except weather, the AI features and cloud sync works with no network at all. Nothing requires you to sign in.
AI, on your key
Garden Buddy runs on Claude Sonnet 5 with nine tools — placing plants, clearing and organising beds, answering schedule questions. It calls the API straight from your browser with a key you supply; it never touches a server of ours.
